Privacy Policy for Jigsaw Puzzle Kids

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Jigsaw Puzzle Kids ("the App") is a jigsaw puzzle game for children, published on Google Play by name-is-jayant ("we", "us" or "our"). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, no accounts, no chat, no camera or microphone access, and no location access. We do not ask children for their name, email, photos or any other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following on the device only:

  • Puzzle progress (completed puzzles, stars earned and unlocked categories)
  • Preferences (day/night theme, sound on/off, and whether onboarding has been seen)

This data never leaves the device through us. If Android's built-in backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game progress in your Google account backup. That backup is managed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information from the device.

3. Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, a Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDK. An ad is shown only when the user taps to watch a short video, to earn an extra hint or unlock a puzzle category early. There are no banner ads or pop-up ads during play.

Because the App is directed at children:

  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and as under the age of consent (GDPR).
  • Ads are limited to G-rated (general audience) content.
  • The App does not use the Android Advertising ID; the App removes that permission.
  • Ads are not personalized, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process limited technical information such as IP address, device type, operating system version and app version. Google handles this information under its own policies and its requirements for child-directed ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob's child-directed treatment.

4. App updates

The App uses the Google Play In-App Updates library to tell you when a newer version is available. This check goes through the Google Play Store app on the device. We receive no information from it.

5. Internet use

All puzzles work offline. An internet connection is used only to load optional reward videos and to check for app updates through Google Play.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information has been shared with us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only third-party service in the App is Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above.

8. Data security and deletion

Game progress stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
