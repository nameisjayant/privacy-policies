Privacy Policy for Doctor Report Maker

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Doctor Report Maker ("the app", "we", "us") is built by Jayant Kumar for doctors and clinics to create prescriptions, lab reports and appointments on their Android device. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: the app collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal or patient information. The app has no account system, no servers, and does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Doctor profile: name, qualification, registration number, clinic name, phone number, address, profile photo, clinic logo and signature.
  • Patient and clinical records: patient names, age or date of birth, gender, phone numbers, addresses, complaints, diagnoses, vitals, medicines, advice, follow-up dates and lab report results.
  • Appointments: patient names, contact details, dates and times.
  • App preferences: theme, chosen PDF template and onboarding status.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. We have no access to it.

3. PDFs you download or share

  • When you download a PDF, it is saved to your device's Downloads/DoctorReportMaker folder (or the app's documents folder on older Android versions). Other apps with access to your files may be able to read it.
  • When you share a PDF, it is sent through Android's share sheet to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies. You are responsible for sharing patient information appropriately.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): only to tell you a PDF finished downloading; tapping the notification opens the file. You can deny this and the app still works.
  • Photo picker: when you choose a logo, signature or profile photo, Android's system photo picker gives the app access to just the image you select. No broad storage permission is requested.

5. Backups

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your records are not copied to Google Drive or any other cloud service. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all records are permanently deleted. Download the PDFs you need to keep.

6. Third-party services

The app has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs. It uses the Google Play In-App Updates library, which asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version is available. No patient or profile data is involved. Google's handling of Play Store data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data security and deletion

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read the app's private storage. For extra protection, use a screen lock on your device. You can delete individual records inside the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for medical professionals and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Medical disclaimer

Doctor Report Maker is a documentation tool. It does not diagnose, interpret results or provide medical advice. Reference ranges are typical adult values and may vary by laboratory.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
