Privacy Policy for World Festivals

Effective date: October 2, 2026

World Festivals (the “App”) lets you explore the countries of the world and the festivals they celebrate. This policy explains what information the App handles. In short:

  • No account, sign-in or personal information is required.
  • The App does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.
  • The internet is used only to download country flag images.

1. Information the App stores

All country and festival information is bundled inside the App. The App saves only the following on your device:

WhatWhy
Whether you have finished the welcome screensSo the introduction is shown only once
Downloaded flag imagesKept in a temporary cache so flags load faster and use less data

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see or access it.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect or process:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details
  • Location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics
  • Your searches or the countries and festivals you view

3. Permissions

The App requests only the Internet permission, which it uses to download country flag images. It does not request any other permissions.

4. Flag images (third-party service)

Country flag images are downloaded from flagcdn.com, a public flag image service. As with any website, when the App requests an image, that service receives standard technical information needed to deliver it, such as your device's IP address and the name of the image requested. No personal information, account details or usage data is sent by the App. If an image cannot be downloaded (for example, when you are offline), the App shows the country's flag emoji instead.

5. Sharing of information

The developer does not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because the App does not collect it in the first place.

6. Backups, data retention and deletion

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's small settings file (whether you have finished the welcome screens) in your device's backup to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google, not by the developer, and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can delete everything the App has stored at any time by going to Settings → Apps → World Festivals → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

7. Security

The App's data is kept in its private storage on your device, which Android isolates from other apps. Flag images are downloaded over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal information, no data about children is gathered or stored by the developer.

9. Accuracy of festival information

Festival details are provided for learning and general interest. Many festivals follow lunar or religious calendars, so their dates change from year to year. Please check local sources before making travel or other plans.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the App does not collect personal data and anything it stores stays on your device, you can exercise these rights directly through your device settings as described above.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
