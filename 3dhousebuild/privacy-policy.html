Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or sell any personal information.
  • There's no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Your designs are saved only on your device.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the 3D House Build Android app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is developed by Jayant Kumar. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no user accounts and no servers of its own. We cannot see your designs or anything else you do in the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your designs: each design's name, room size, furniture, floor and wall finishes, kept in a private database on your device.
  • App settings: your theme (light or dark), whether you've finished the welcome screens, and when the app last suggested an update.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete a design at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything the app has stored.

Files you create, share or import

  • PDF exports and backups: when you download a PDF or back up your designs, the file is saved on your device (in Downloads/3DHouseBuild, or the app's own folder on older Android versions).
  • Sharing: when you share a picture or a design file, Android's share menu opens and you choose the app you send it to. What happens to it next is covered by that app's privacy policy.
  • Importing: when you open a design file shared with you, the app reads only that file and adds the design to your device. Files that aren't 3D House Build designs are refused.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to tell you when a design PDF has finished downloading. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.
  • Vibration: used for small haptic taps, for example when a piece snaps onto a wall.
  • Internet: this permission is included by the libraries the app is built with. The app does not use it to send your designs or any personal information anywhere. All features work fully offline.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Third-party services

  • Google Play app updates: the app uses Google Play's in-app update feature to let you know when a new version is available. That check is handled by the Google Play Store on your device and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup: if you have turned on device backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your backup to your Google account. This is controlled by you and by Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data security

Your designs are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Files you choose to export or share are stored wherever you save or send them, so please keep them as safe as you would any other file.

Your choices

  • Delete any design from your designs list.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove everything it has stored.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
