Privacy Policy for Expenz

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expenz mobile application (the "App"), developed by Jayant Kumar ("I", "me" or "the developer"), handles your information. Expenz is built to be private by design: it works fully offline and does not collect, transmit or share your personal data.

In short
  • No account, sign-up or login is required.
  • The App does not request internet access and does not send your data anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • The developer has no access to your data.

1. Information the App stores

To provide its features, the App saves the information you choose to enter in a private database on your device. This includes:

FeatureWhat is stored on your device
ExpensesAmount, category and date of each expense
BudgetsMonthly budget amount for each month
InvestmentsAmount, investment type, your note and date
GoalsGoal text, details, completion status and dates
Split expensesGroup name, number of people sharing, shared costs by type, and whether the group is cleared
App settingsTheme preference (light/dark), whether onboarding tips were shown, and when you were last prompted about an app update

This information never leaves your device through the App. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see, access or recover it.

The split expenses feature only records a group name and the number of people. It does not access your contacts or ask for the names, phone numbers or other details of the people you share costs with.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect or process:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details
  • Bank, card, UPI or other financial account credentials
  • Location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics

3. Permissions

Expenz does not request any sensitive Android permissions, including internet access. It cannot connect to the internet on its own.

4. Google Play in-app updates

The App uses the Google Play In-App Updates library so it can let you know when a newer version is available. This check is handled by the Google Play Store app already installed on your device, not by Expenz. Expenz does not send any of your personal or financial data as part of this process. Google's handling of information through the Play Store is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of information

The developer does not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because the App does not collect it in the first place.

6. Backups, data retention and deletion

Cloud backup is turned off for the App, so your records are not copied to online storage by the App. Your data stays on your device until you delete it.

You are in full control of your data:

  • You can edit or delete individual entries inside the App at any time.
  • You can clear old months of expenses from within the App.
  • You can delete all App data by going to Settings → Apps → Expenz → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting it or uninstalling the App removes it permanently, and it cannot be recovered by the developer.

7. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, which Android isolates from other apps. The security of this data also depends on your device's own protections, such as a screen lock. I recommend keeping your device secured and up to date.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the App collects no personal information, no data about children is gathered or stored by the developer.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because all data is stored only on your device and never reaches the developer, you can exercise these rights directly within the App or through your device settings as described above.

10. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
